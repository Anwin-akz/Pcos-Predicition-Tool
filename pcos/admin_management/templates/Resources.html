Educational Resources on PCOS

What is PCOS?

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a common hormonal disorder among women of reproductive age. Symptoms include irregular periods, excess androgen, and polycystic ovaries.

Common Symptoms

Irregular or prolonged menstrual cycles

Excess facial or body hair (hirsutism)

Weight gain

Acne or oily skin

Thinning hair or hair loss on the scalp

Management and Treatment

PCOS can be managed through lifestyle changes, medication, and other treatments. Consult a healthcare provider for personalized advice.
