ABOUT US

Welcome to the PCOS Prediction Tool, a cutting-edge platform dedicated to assisting in the early detection and management of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS). Our mission is to leverage the power of artificial intelligence (AI) to provide accessible, accurate, and transparent diagnostic support to patients and healthcare providers.

Our tool combines the latest advancements in machine learning and deep learning to analyze health data and ultrasound images, offering real-time insights into PCOS risk. By integrating Explainable AI (XAI) techniques, we ensure that every prediction is interpretable and trustworthy, empowering users to make informed health decisions.

At the heart of our project is a commitment to improving healthcare accessibility and outcomes, particularly for those in underserved areas. Whether you're seeking early detection, medical insights, or enhanced diagnostic transparency, our platform is here to support you every step of the way.
